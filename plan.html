How this works

Equal risk, not equal money. Each trade is sized so that if its stop-loss is hit, you lose the same rupee amount — your chosen % of budget. A volatile stock therefore gets fewer shares than a steady one.

Deploy as signals appear. We only publish a setup when the reward is at least twice the risk, which is usually a handful of stocks a day and sometimes none at all. You are not meant to invest the whole budget today — keep the rest in cash and add positions as they qualify.

One sector cap. No more than two positions from the same sector, so one bad sector cannot take out your month.
